Many accounting teams inherit invoice problems they didn't create. What should be simple data validation turns the invoice management process into a cleanup operation: matching, coding, chasing approvals, reconciling discrepancies, and getting payment out before the vendor calls. That cleanup is expensive, especially at scale.
